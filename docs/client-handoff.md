## The basics

Your site is built from **content blocks**. Think of them like Lego pieces. You can:

- Reorder sections by dragging
- Hide a section without deleting it
- Edit text by clicking on it
- Swap images
- Update buttons and links

You **cannot** break the design. Layout, fonts, colors, and spacing are locked.

## Common tasks

### Edit a homepage section

1. Open the Studio
2. Click **Home Page**
3. Find the section in the list (Hero, Features, etc.)
4. Click to expand and edit
5. Click **Publish** when done

Changes go live within seconds.

### Add a blog post

1. Click **Blog Posts**
2. Click the **+** in the top right
3. Fill in title, excerpt, cover image, and body
4. Set the **Published At** date (this controls sort order on the blog index)
5. Click **Publish**

### Update site-wide info (logo, footer, contact email)

1. Click **Site Settings**
2. Find what you need (logo, social links, contact info, etc.)
3. Edit and **Publish**

### Use Visual Editing

Click the **Presentation** tab in the Studio. You'll see your live site. Click on any text or image to edit it directly. This is the fastest way to make small tweaks.

## Tips

- **Save often.** The Studio auto-saves drafts, but only **Publish** makes it live.
- **Image sizes:** any size works. Large images are fine; the site optimizes them automatically.
- **Alt text:** always fill in the "Alt Text" field on images. It helps with SEO and accessibility.
- **Character limits:** if a field warns you about length, the warning is there to keep the design looking right. Trim it down.

## Things you should NOT do

- Don't delete the Home Page document. If you don't need it, hide its sections instead.
- Don't delete Site Settings.
- Don't change the slug of an existing page without telling me. URLs that change break inbound links and SEO.
